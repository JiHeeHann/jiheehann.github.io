Research focus.

01

Venous thromboembolism

Understanding the occurrence, determinants, outcomes, and prevention of venous thromboembolism across populations and healthcare settings.

02

Real-world evidence

Using routinely collected healthcare data, cohort studies, and modern epidemiological methods to generate reliable clinical evidence.

03

Hemostasis & population health

Investigating genetic, biomarker, and population-level determinants of hypercoagulability and cardiometabolic disease.

Methods & data.

A

Clinical & healthcare data

Real-world healthcare databases, cohort and registry studies, and target-trial emulation.

B

Genetic epidemiology

Genome-wide analyses, risk prediction, and investigations of genetic mechanisms underlying thrombotic disease.

C

Biomarkers & multi-omics

Coagulation factors, thrombin generation, proteomics, and metabolomics to study disease pathways.
