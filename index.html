About

Connecting population health with molecular insight.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Clinical Epidemiology at Leiden University Medical Center. My work brings together clinical, genetic, and population-level data to better understand thrombosis and cardiometabolic disease.

I aim to develop an independent research programme that clarifies how hypercoagulability arises, whom it affects, and how this knowledge can improve prevention and prognosis across different clinical settings.

  • Based at Leiden University Medical Center
  • Discipline Clinical Epidemiology
  • Current focus Hemostasis & cardiometabolic disease

Research

Questions that guide my work.

I investigate determinants and dysregulation of hemostatic factors using large-scale clinical, genetic, and omics data.

01

Venous thromboembolism

Understanding risk factors, prognosis, and variation in venous thromboembolism across populations and care settings.

02

Hemostasis & omics

Mapping genetic, proteomic, and metabolic pathways that influence coagulation and hypercoagulability.

03

Cardiometabolic health

Examining how coagulation, lifestyle, and molecular mechanisms relate to metabolic and cardiovascular outcomes.

  • Real-world healthcare data
  • Cohort & registry studies
  • Genetic epidemiology
  • Multi-omics
  • Risk prediction
