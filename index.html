About

Clinical evidence, seen at population scale.

Jihee Han is a clinical epidemiologist working at the intersection of venous thrombosis, real-world evidence, and population health.

Her research combines large-scale healthcare databases, prospective cohorts, genetic and biomarker data, and modern epidemiological methods to understand why thrombotic disease occurs and how clinical evidence can be improved.

Background

She is a postdoctoral researcher in Clinical Epidemiology at Leiden University Medical Center. Her work builds on training in clinical epidemiology, nutritional science, genetics, and biotechnology.
