About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Clinical Epidemiology at Leiden University Medical Center. My research focuses on venous thromboembolism, hypercoagulability, cardiometabolic disease, and the use of real-world healthcare data.

I am particularly interested in understanding how clinical, genetic, and population-level factors contribute to thrombosis risk and prognosis.
